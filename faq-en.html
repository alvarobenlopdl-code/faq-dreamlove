Customer payment and wallet balance Payments

When a customer pays in your store, that money goes to your bank account and is not automatically deposited into your Dreamlove wallet balance. If you want orders to be processed automatically on our platform, you must have sufficient wallet balance beforehand before the customer completes their payment.

SEO and product URLs SEO

Our synchronization does not depend on public URLs, but on the product’s internal identifier in Shopify. This lets you adjust SEO (titles, descriptions, URLs, metafields) as needed without affecting the integration. SEO management must be handled by the client.

Can I change prices, collections, or SEO after import? Catalog

Prices: Yes, you can modify them. Products arrive with a recommended profit margin, but you are free to adjust it. You can use third-party apps for bulk changes; we do not provide support for such apps, so we recommend consulting their official documentation and tutorial videos.

SEO: You can manage titles, descriptions, metafields, and URLs in Shopify or with third-party apps. We do not provide SEO support; we suggest reviewing official documentation and training resources. Note that our synchronization does not depend on the public URL, but on the product’s internal identifier.

Collections: You can add new collections for your strategy (e.g., “New Arrivals”). Do not modify the base collections created by the plugin, except the images if you want to customize them.

I already have Dreamlove products in my store; could there be duplicates? Catalog

Yes. If you keep previous products, there may be duplicates or orphan variants. We recommend removing existing products related to Dreamlove before performing the import.

Which location should I use to prepare orders? Shipping

How many languages can I have in my Shopify store? Language

For free, Shopify allows up to 3 languages via the Translate & Adapt app: one primary language (it must match the product import language) and two additional languages with automatic translation. If you want more languages, you can use third-party apps from the Shopify marketplace; note that we do not provide support for third-party apps.

How do I manage legal policies and the return policy in my store? Policies

Yes, you can add and edit legal content directly from your Shopify admin:

  1. Legal policies: go to Settings → Policies to configure the privacy policy, returns, terms and conditions, etc.
  2. Additional pages: from Online Store → Pages you can create sections such as “About us”, “Frequently Asked Questions”, or “Legal Notice”.

Regarding the return policy, you can take ours as a reference, but you must adapt it at your own discretion, deciding whether to offer more or fewer guarantees.

It is important that your store’s final policy clearly explains the procedure a customer must follow to return an order, without including internal references to Dreamlove. You should only communicate specific data to the customer once the return process has been approved.

More information in the policies guide.
