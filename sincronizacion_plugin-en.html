  1. Plugin Purchase

    The Dreamlove Shopify Plugin imports and synchronizes products, descriptions, images, prices, and stock in your store.

    Purchase options
    • Website: on our website you can purchase our plugin. You must accept the terms and conditions of use.
    • Billing: you can contact our Administration department to issue the plugin invoice directly.
    Buy Administration Contact

  2. App Installation

    Once you have purchased the plugin, you must install the Dreamlove app in your Shopify store.

    Installation steps
    1. Access the Shopify admin with a user who has permissions to install apps.
    2. Open the installation link or the App Store listing.
    3. Review the permissions and click Install app.
    4. You will be redirected to the plugin panel. The technical team will perform the initial configuration to activate the app.

    During installation Shopify will show you the permissions the plugin needs to operate:

    • Products Read/Write (products, variants, images, collections).
    • Orders Read (to link incidents and automated flows).
    • Inventory and locations read access.
    • Webhooks management (to keep stock and prices up to date).

    Install

  3. Location Implementation

    Once the catalog has been imported, you must define in Shopify the order fulfillment location. This location indicates where products are managed and shipped to your customers from.

    Recommended configuration
    • Go to Settings → Shipping and delivery in your Shopify admin.
    • In the Order fulfillment locations section, Dreamlove Fulfillment Service should appear as the main option.
    • Verify that your Shop location is correctly configured (for example, “Spain”).
    • If you need to manage additional shipping zones, use the Add shipping zone option.
    • Need more details about rates and shipping zones? See the guide in this section of the configuration manual .
    Importance of the location

    By linking Dreamlove Fulfillment Service as the location, all orders for synchronized products will be shipped directly via our logistics service. This ensures that stock, preparation, and tracking are updated automatically in your store.

  4. Language and store consistency

    The import language must match your store’s default language. Keeping this consistency prevents content mismatches.

    • In Shopify, check Settings → Store language and verify it matches.
    • If you want to sell in multiple languages, set up translations with Translate & Adapt after the base import.
    • If you want to have more languages than the application allows Translate & Adapt in your store, you will need to purchase a third-party application.
    • Please note that we do not offer technical support for third-party applications. We recommend reviewing the official documentation.

  5. Collaborator request

    To assist you, we need collaborator access. Please provide the domain of your store that ends in myshopify.com.

    Where can I see my myshopify.com domain?
    1. In the Shopify admin, at the top left, click your store name.
    2. In the menu, go to Settings. In the header or in Domains you will see the identifier yourstore.myshopify.com.
    3. Send us exactly that domain so we can send the collaboration request.

  6. Product structure and information

    The plugin brings the essential information to sell and keep items up to date. Keep the following in mind:

    Product import
    • Recommended retail price (RRP) visible to the end customer.
    • SKU and variant data (size/color, etc.).
    • Stock and inventory by location (according to configuration).
    • Images and product marketing descriptions.
    • Base categories/collections for initial organization.
    SEO

    The plugin’s focus is operational; it does not perform advanced SEO optimization. You can complement later with apps or theme adjustments (titles/meta, rich snippets, etc.). SEO management must be handled by the client.

    Categories and collections
    • The base categories are created/updated to organize the catalog.
    • You can add new collections tailored to your strategy (e.g., “New Arrivals”, “Trends”).
    • Do not modify the base structure generated by the plugin, so as not to break future synchronizations.

  7. Automatic orders and wallet

    For orders to be processed automatically from your store to Dreamlove, you need to have sufficient balance in your wallet.

    Requirements
    • Plugin installed and configured.
    • Automatic orders enabled in the plugin panel.
    • Wallet balance with enough credit to cover the order amount and shipping.
    • Available products with stock and in the catalog.
    • Online Store Channel, orders placed through the Online Store channel.
      We strongly recommend that you do not install third-party applications that manage, modify, or interfere with the order flow, as they could disrupt the proper functioning of the plugin.
    How to top up the wallet
    1. Access your Dreamlove customer area or the link provided by support.
    2. Open the Express order / Wallet balance section.
    3. Add sufficient credit.
    Flow when an order comes in
    1. The customer buys in your store and the order is created in Shopify.
    2. The plugin detects it and sends it to Dreamlove if there is sufficient balance and the configuration is correct.
    3. Stock is reserved; when it is prepared, it is updated and set to the “Prepared” status.
    4. Once the order has the “Prepared” status, the tracking number is added to the order.
    5. The customer receives status and tracking updates.

    If the order has been blocked due to insufficient wallet balance, additional support hours will be required if the client wants us to process the order. If you do not want us to process the order blocked due to wallet balance, you can manage it manually from our Store.

    No balance: if there isn’t enough credit, the order will remain pending. Top up your wallet balance and contact the technical team to process the order. If the wallet balance increases, blocked orders will not be processed automatically; the technical team must review and process them manually, and additional support hours will be required.
    Payment methods in Shopify

    In addition, you must configure payment methods in your Shopify so orders are properly confirmed (Shopify Payments, PayPal, or other providers). Without an active payment method, orders may remain unpaid or pending and will not be shipped automatically.
